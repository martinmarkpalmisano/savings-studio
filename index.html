LEARN / HOW THE CALCULATOR WORKS

Calculate net savings interest in Italy

Savings Studio helps you estimate the interest you earn on a savings account (conto deposito or conto remunerato). Enter your opening balance, the annual interest rate and the duration. Choose daily or monthly payments to see a dated statement, an evolving balance and downloadable CSV data.

Daily vs monthly interest

Daily mode estimates interest using the annual gross rate divided by 365 (366 in leap years), with net interest credited in whole cents. Monthly mode converts AER into an effective monthly rate. The same headline percentage can produce different results because the two modes use different rate conventions.

Single-rate or tiered savings

Switch off tiered interest to apply one annual rate to your entire balance. Switch it on to give the first portion of your savings one rate and only the amount above the specified threshold another rate. Your settings remain editable.

Italian taxes and account costs

The tool defaults to 26% withholding on deposit interest. You may also estimate applicable stamp duty and an optional monthly subscription, such as a paid banking plan. Subscription costs are tracked separately from the savings balance so the comparison reflects overall economic profit.

Fixed-term deposits and coupons

Choose fixed-term deposit to calculate simple interest on locked principal without automatic reinvestment. Set a term in months, select monthly, quarterly, annual or maturity-only payouts and see net coupons after interest tax. Optional annual stamp duty is treated as an external cost.

Privacy and exports

Calculations run locally in your browser. No bank login, personal financial credentials or external account connection is required. Export daily or monthly projections to CSV and reuse scenarios on your phone or computer.

How is interest calculated? +

Monthly mode treats inputs as AER and uses (1 + AER / 100)^(1/12) − 1. Daily mode treats the inputs as gross annual p.a. rates, calculates daily accrued interest using the actual calendar year (365/366 days), withholds 26% by default and carries fractional net cents until €0.01 can be credited. These are different rate conventions; the same number may produce different annual results.

Gross interest is rounded to cents for each tier. The configured Italian withholding tax (default 26%) is deducted when interest is credited. Only net interest compounds; any monthly top-up is added at month-end.

If enabled, annual stamp duty is estimated at the selected rate (default 0.20%) on the year-end balance, with a €1 minimum, and deducted every 12 months. Actual bank charges depend on statement timing and product terms. This is an illustrative estimate, not a tax determination.

When the optional monthly plan fee is enabled, it is assumed to be paid outside the savings account. The account balance and future interest are therefore unchanged, but total profit and monthly net benefit are reduced by each subscription payment.

This is an illustrative forecast, not a bank statement. Bank payment dates, taxes rounded to cents, rate conventions, promotional periods, and fees may differ from this projection. Switching currencies changes the symbol, not the numeric amounts.
