How is interest calculated? +

Each portion of the opening balance earns its own annual equivalent rate (AER). We convert each AER to an effective monthly rate using (1 + AER / 100)^(1/12) − 1.

Gross interest is rounded to cents for each tier. The configured Italian withholding tax (default 26%) is deducted when interest is credited. Only net interest compounds; any monthly top-up is added at month-end.

If enabled, annual stamp duty is estimated at the selected rate (default 0.20%) on the year-end balance, with a €1 minimum, and deducted every 12 months. Actual bank charges depend on statement timing and product terms. This is an illustrative estimate, not a tax determination.

This is an illustrative forecast, not a bank statement. Banks can accrue interest daily, use different rounding rules, change advertised rates, and apply different rates, taxes or fees. Switching currencies changes the symbol, not the numeric amounts.
